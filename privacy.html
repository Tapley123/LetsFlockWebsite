Privacy Policy

Let’s Flock! · Last updated 1 November 2026

Let’s Flock! is a social VR game for Meta Quest, made by Tapley Games (“we” or “us”). This policy explains what the game and this website collect, why, who else handles it, and how you can see or delete it. We’ve tried to keep it easy to read. If you’re under 18, it’s a good one to read with a parent or guardian.

The short version

  • Your Meta account signs you in. We use your Meta user ID and username to run your game account and save your progress.
  • Voice chat is live. It goes to the players in your room. We don’t record it or keep it.
  • We never ask for your real name, email address or payment details in the game.
  • No ads, and we don’t sell your data.
  • You’re in charge. Ask us at any time to show you your data or delete it: letsflockvr+privacy@gmail.com.
  • Let’s Flock! is for ages 13 and over.

What we don’t collect

The game doesn’t collect your real name, email address, phone number, home address, payment details or precise location. It doesn’t use your headset’s cameras, eye tracking or face tracking, and it has no advertising or advertising IDs. Meta handles your Meta account separately, under its own privacy policy.

2. Why we use it

We only use your information for the reasons below. If you’re in the UK or the EU, the law asks us to give a legal reason for each one, so we’ve listed those too.

What we doOur legal reason
Run the game: sign you in, save your progress, connect you with other players, carry voice chat, power friends features and deliver purchases It’s needed to give you the game you asked for (contract)
Keep the game fair and safe: check you own the game, cap rewards at what’s possible, look into reports and ban accounts Our legitimate interest, and all players’ interest, in a fair and safe game
Fix bugs and keep our servers secure Our legitimate interest in the game working properly and safely
Keep purchase records and answer lawful requests We have to by law (legal obligation)

We don’t use your information for advertising. We don’t sell it, and we don’t share it for anyone’s advertising. We don’t make automated decisions about you that have a legal or similarly serious effect.

3. What other players can see

Let’s Flock! is a social game, so some things are visible to other people:

  • your Meta username, on your name tag, on boards and on leaderboards
  • your bird, its look and how it moves
  • your voice, if you’re nearby and not muted
  • your scores, your place on the Most Wanted board, and the Gossip magpie’s news about who pooped on whom
  • for your friends: whether you’re online, and where you are so they can join you

Please take care. Other players can hear what you say and could record their own screen, so never share personal details such as your full name, school or address. If your Meta username includes your real name, you can change it in your Meta account.

5. Where it’s processed

These companies run servers around the world, including in the United States and the European Union. Photon connects you to a server region near you, or to a friend’s region when you join them. This means your information may be handled outside your country. When it leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on our providers’ safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved for these transfers.

7. Your choices and rights

In the game

  • Your microphone: you can mute yourself, or say no to the microphone permission. You can still play, but nobody will hear you.
  • Other players: you can mute, block or report anyone from the pause menu. You can also report through the Meta button.
  • Who you play with: you can open a friends-only nest to play with just your friends.

Your legal rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK, the EU and US states such as California, you may have the right to:

  • see the information we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • have it corrected
  • have it deleted
  • object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it

To use any of these rights, email letsflockvr+privacy@gmail.com with your Meta username. It’s free, and we’ll reply within one month. A parent or guardian can ask on behalf of a child. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to a data protection regulator, such as the ICO in the UK or your local authority in the EU.

8. How to delete your data

  1. Email letsflockvr+privacy@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. Include your Meta username. We may ask a question or two to check the account is yours.
  3. We’ll delete your data and confirm by email within one month.

This removes your game account, progress, looks, items and Golden Eggs, and clears your leaderboard scores. It can’t be undone. We’ll keep only what the law requires, such as purchase records, and what we need for safety, such as a record of a ban.

Removing the game from your headset doesn’t delete this data, so you’ll need to ask us. If your Meta account is deleted, we’ll delete the game data linked to it once we’re told. Your Meta account, and what Meta holds about it, are managed in your Meta account settings.

9. Children and teens

Let’s Flock! is for ages 13 and over. It isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If we learn that a child under 13 has been playing, we’ll delete their information.

If you’re a parent or guardian and think your child under 13 has played, please email letsflockvr+privacy@gmail.com and we’ll remove it. Players can also report someone who’s underage from inside the game.

The game has open voice chat with other players. If your teenager plays, it’s worth showing them how to mute, block and report. You can contact us at any time with questions about your child’s information.

10. Keeping it safe

Sign-ins, saves and purchases travel over encrypted connections. Meta confirms who you are before our server signs you in, and our secret keys stay on our servers, not in the game. No online service is perfectly secure, so we can’t promise nothing will ever go wrong. If something does happen that puts you at risk, we’ll tell you and the regulators as the law requires.

11. This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and it has no forms. Its fonts, pictures and video all load from this site. GitHub hosts it and may log visitors’ IP addresses to keep its service secure.

Links to the Meta store, Discord, TikTok and YouTube take you to other companies’ sites, which have their own privacy policies. Discord is for ages 13 and over.

12. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If it’s a big change, we’ll also let players know in the game or on our Discord.
